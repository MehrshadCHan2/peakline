The actual problem

Everyone in your niche is starting to look the same.

Same zooms. Same stock B-roll. Same three sound effects. Most short-form editing reaches for volume because volume is easy to show, and the thing that made you worth watching gets buried under it.

A short has about a second to earn the next second. That's won by the first line, the cut on the pause, and the moment you stop performing and just say the thing. It isn't won by a caption template.

I work the other way. I find the strongest 30–60 seconds in what you already said, and I cut everything that stands in front of it.

Before you ask

The questions people are too polite to ask first.

What if the first edit isn’t what I pictured?
That’s what the free short is for: you see how I think on your own footage before you commit to anything. After that, we agree a written style sheet, so “what you pictured” is on paper before I cut. If an edit misses something we agreed in writing, I fix it at no cost. That’s my mistake, not a revision.
Will my shorts end up looking like everyone else’s?
No templates, no preset packs, no house style pasted over your channel. If a zoom, a sound effect or a caption doesn’t serve the idea, it isn’t there.
Will I have to give the same notes every week?
No. Every note goes into your style sheet, so the same correction never has to happen twice. Most people start by directing and, within a month, are just approving.
How many revisions are included?
One full round on every edit. You leave timestamped comments and I work through all of them together. Rounds after that are billed by the time they take, and I tell you the estimate before I start.
How do I know you won’t go quiet?
I work with a small number of clients at a time, deliberately. You get a delivery schedule when we start, and if anything is going to move you hear it from me before the deadline, not after.
Do you edit long-form too?
Yes. A long-form first cut takes 5–7 days on a monthly plan, 7–9 days as a one-off. If you publish both, I cut the shorts from the same session.
How does payment work?
Invoiced in USD, paid by PayPal or crypto. Monthly plans are paid at the start of the month. You get an invoice every time.
Will you show my videos in your portfolio?
Yes, and I’m upfront about it because it’s a condition of working together: I show short excerpts of the work I did. Never before your video is public. The video itself is yours, completely and permanently.
Have you edited for someone like me before?
Straight answer: most of my client volume came from a hundred-plus American practice owners, dentists through a marketing agency, publishing week after week under their own names. My own channel is about AI and content creation, so if that’s your world, I already speak it. Short-form for creators and founders is the only work I take on now.
Am I talking to a person or a company?
Just me. One editor, one point of contact. You can see my face and hear how I talk before you commit to anything; it’s the video at the top of this page.
