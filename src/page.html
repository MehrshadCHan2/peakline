FAQ

Questions clinics ask.

Who films the videos?{{PLUS}}

You do, on your phone. In the full content system we send the scripts and guide a 60-minute filming session each month; that hour gives us your shorts for the month.

What do you need from us?{{PLUS}}

A link to the raw video (Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer). Your logo and brand colours help, but they're optional.

Have you worked with clinics like ours?{{PLUS}}

Yes. We've edited 600+ medical videos for 13+ clinics, made with a partner agency. The clinics in our work section were that agency's clients.

Who edits the videos?{{PLUS}}

A small team trained on our editing system for dental and medical video. Every short is checked before it reaches you.

Why not just hire our own editor?{{PLUS}}

An in-house editor costs a full salary, needs managing, and leaves when they leave. With us you get a tested editing system, a quality check on every short, and a team that keeps delivering when one person is sick or on holiday.

How much does it cost?{{PLUS}}

It depends on how many shorts you need. We send a quote after your free short, so you can judge the quality first.

Can we ask for changes?{{PLUS}}

Yes. One round of changes is included on every short.
